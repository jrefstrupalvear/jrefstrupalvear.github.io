Workflow Field Guide

To identify automation opportunities, sample completed cases from one team’s queue. Look for manual steps that repeat, measure the staff time involved, and check that a rule can handle the ordinary cases. Subtract the time people would still spend on review, then choose a small pilot with a clear stop condition.

Example pilot decision

In the hypothetical invoice-routing example, removing 270 minutes of work and adding 120 minutes of review leaves 150 staff minutes recovered per week. The pilot advances if it can recover at least 60 minutes per week without routing an invoice to the wrong reviewer. A wrong route triggers a review of the rule.
